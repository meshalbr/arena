          Rules of the game

          The score

          Each stock's last 2 years are split into the part the whole market explains and the stock's own part. The score is the stock's own gain over months 12 to 1, divided by how bumpy it was. The latest month is skipped.

          The turns

          Day 5 plays on the 5th trading day of each month, Day 10 on the 10th, Day 15 on the 15th, and Month end on the last. Splitting the money four ways means no single day's luck decides the year.

          The power-up

          Leverage 3x while SPY closed above its 200-day average the day before, else 1x. Margin interest about 6% a year on the loan, 0.10% per step of leverage changed.

          The moves

          After drawing, a player trades its pile so each of its 6 cards holds one sixth of it, a third per stock. Stocks no card names any more are sold, and new names are bought.

          The rivals

          The players talk, gloat and sulk like real rivals, but that is only for fun. Monthly trophies in the replay go to whoever gained the most that month, as a share of its own money.

          Real-world rules

          A normal US account cannot do 3x: Reg T caps buying at 2x, and 3x needs portfolio margin ($110,000+). This page is a what-if only. Your own risk rules also block margin today. Back-tests: worst drop -54%; in a 2008-style crash this loses most of the money. Fake money, not advice.
