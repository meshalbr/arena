    How to read this page

    In play: a stock trading far more than usual in the first 5 minutes. Opening range: the high and low of the first 5 minutes. VWAP: the average price paid today, weighted by volume. Stop: the price where a losing trade is closed. R: one R is the amount risked on a trade; +2R means it made twice what it risked. Profit factor: money won divided by money lost; above 1 is profitable. Fills are simulated at the next minute's opening price, a little worse than the market, with US selling fees. No real money and no broker orders are involved.
