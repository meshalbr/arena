❓ New to trading? How to read this page
The big number
What $1,000 of pretend money is worth now. Green = you made money, red = you lost.
The market (SPY)
A fund that owns the 500 biggest US companies. If the formula can't beat it, just buying SPY would be easier.
Players
The $1,000 is split between a few players. Each one picks the 3 strongest stocks on its own day of the month.
BUY / ADD / TRIM / SELL
Buy a new stock / buy more of one / sell part / sell all of it.
Replay
Re-runs the past day by day so you can watch what the formula would have done.
Real exam
Pick a start day. A fresh $1,000 runs from there with real-life costs taken off.
Costs
Slippage (you never get the exact price), small exchange fees, and 30% US tax on dividends.
Tug of war
The white block slides right when the formula is beating the market, left when it is losing to it.
Pain meter
How far the account is below the best it has ever been. Every strategy spends most of its time below its top.
Guess game
The replay stops each month and asks you: up or down? It shows how hard the market is to predict.
Hover
Point at a character or at the race chart to see its numbers. Drag on the chart to move through time.
Keys

          Your three

          Rules of the game

          The score

          Each stock's return over the 6 months that end one month ago. The top 7 are held.

          The turns

          Once a month, on the last trading day's close; the orders fill at the next open.

          The moves

          Stocks that fall out of the top 7 are sold; newcomers get an equal slice; stayers are left alone.

          The rivals

          The Rocket against the Index. Monthly trophies go to whoever gained more that month.

          Real-broker rules

          Orders are decided on a close and fill at the next open as market orders, 0.10% worse than the open price. Sells pay the SEC fee ($20.60 per $1M) and the FINRA fee ($0.000195 a share, at least 1 cent). Dividends arrive in cash with 30% US tax withheld (no US-Saudi treaty). Splits adjust the shares. About 10% stays in cash, no stock over 25% of the account, every order through your risk check. Fake money: no broker is ever contacted.
